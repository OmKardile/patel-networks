What it does

Commerce features that carry their weight

No feature here is decorative — each one answers a specific counter, stockroom or accountant problem.

Catalog that answers back

Facet filters across category, brand, price, resolution, rating and availability — plus debounced autocomplete in the header.

5-step CCTV kit builder

Recorder → cameras bounded by its channels → storage with a retention estimate → power & cable. Compatibility is enforced; the bundle discount applies itself.

Side-by-side compare

Specs, prices and badges in one honest table — the lowest price is marked, not hidden.

OTP sign-in, no passwords

Customers sign in with a phone and a one-time code. Guest carts merge automatically when they do.

Wishlist with price-drop alerts

Saved items watch their own prices and tell the customer when to come back — a quiet re-marketing engine.

Tracking without an account

Any customer can follow an order with just the order number and phone — rate-limited and sanitized.

GST-native checkout

B2B buyers drop a GSTIN and get input-tax-credit invoices; everyone gets CGST/SGST or IGST computed correctly by state.

Reviews that earn trust

Nothing publishes unapproved. Moderation happens in the console; credibility happens on the page.

Server-enforced lifecycle

An order can only move where the state machine allows

Legal transitions live in the database, not in the UI. A staffer simply never sees a button that would corrupt an order.

Fulfillment console

Legal-move-only buttons, serial numbers captured at pack, AWB booking and carrier-scan events — then a CSV when the accountant asks.

Stock Monitor & inventory

A state-tile stock wall with kiosk mode. Staff observe, count and propose; managers approve and apply. Every adjustment carries a reason code and a ledger line.

Books & taxes

A sales chart, tax summary and a GSTR-1 schedule with CSV export — filing stops being a month-end crisis.
